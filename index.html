THE BASICS

Learn the metrics.

A short guide to explaining your project and interpreting the result.

01 / CORE FORMULA

What is engagement rate?

Engagement rate measures the share of an audience that interacted with a piece of content. It is commonly expressed as a percentage.

( Likes + Comments + Shares + Saves )
÷ Followers × 100
02 / FOUNDER'S NOTE

Follower-based rate

Divides interactions by follower count. It is simple to calculate and useful for comparing posts from the same account when follower counts are similar.

Example500 interactions ÷ 10,000 followers = 5%
03 / ALTERNATIVE

Reach-based rate

Divides interactions by the number of unique accounts reached. Use this when reach data is available and you want to understand response among people who saw the content.

Example500 interactions ÷ 8,000 reach = 6.25%
04 / IMPORTANT

Benchmarks are context-dependent

There is no universal “good” rate. Results vary by platform, industry, audience size, format, and campaign objective. Treat the on-screen benchmark as a rough guide, not a grade.

05 / PROJECT FLOW

How this application works

  1. Enter platform and post metrics.
  2. Validate inputs and calculate interactions.
  3. Calculate follower-based rate and, if possible, reach-based rate.
  4. Display an interpretation and save the run locally.
  5. Review history or export results to CSV.
06 / VIVA PREPARATION

What can you explain?

Input: platform, followers, likes, comments, shares, saves, and optional reach.

Output: engagement rate, total interactions, interpretation, and history.

Tools: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, browser localStorage, and CSV export.
